Why this page looks different

demo-shop1.reserve-me.ru and this page are two separate sites in the same running deployment - the platform's actual multi-tenancy boundary, not a cosmetic one. Every row either tenant's visitors or operators can reach is scoped to its own site id; there is no shared conversation queue, no shared operator, no way for one tenant's login to see the other's data. The different color scheme here is deliberate, so that boundary is visible at a glance and not just true underneath.

Try it

Two operator logins are on offer below this card - Demo Shop Two's own, shared with every visitor here, or a private tenant of your own. Pick either, then:

  1. Open the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of this page and send a message.
  2. Open the operator console in a new tab (or a private/incognito window - the console's own Keycloak session is a cookie, so a second identity needs a separate browser context): console.reserve-me.ru - sign in with whichever login you picked, not demo-shop1's.
  3. Find your message in the queue and reply. Then check demo-shop1's own operator login in your original tab or window - it will not see this conversation at all.
